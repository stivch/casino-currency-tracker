Installed

Open Stake or Duel and a small readout appears in the bottom-right corner. Everything below is already configured — this page exists so that the two switches which change what the extension requests are a decision you made rather than one you discover later.

Pin the toolbar icon

Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin this extension. The icon carries the live rate, and your session profit or loss while you are playing — green up, red down — so it is worth having visible.

What leaves your machine

Rate lookups to CoinGecko and exchangerate-api, and nothing else. No account, no analytics, no server of ours — there is nowhere for your data to go. History, settings and session figures stay in the browser's own storage. The optional CoinGecko API key is kept on this machine only, is never synced, and is never handed to the script that runs inside the casino's page.

The two switches worth knowing about

Track session P/L — on Counts your bets from the site's own ledger. On Stake that ledger is the “My Bets” table, already on the page, so it is only watched. Duel has no bet table, so its transaction feed is read instead — that means a request every 15 seconds, but only while a Duel tab is open, visible and focused. Turn this off in Options and nothing is requested on either site.
Read rakeback and VIP progress — off Reads account data the casino's own app already fetches. Watching only, until you also turn on the poll beside it — that one repeats a request to the account API on a timer, from your logged-in session. Casinos' terms restrict automated traffic, and the account that would be flagged is yours. Off unless you decide otherwise.

Limits report, they do not intervene

Wager, loss, win and time limits flag in the overlay and raise a desktop notice when a session crosses one. Nothing is blocked and no bet is stopped — this extension only ever tells you where you stand. The figures it produces are a floor, not an accounting record: the site's own history is authoritative, and nothing here is tax or financial advice.
